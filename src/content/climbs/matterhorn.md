## The plan

Long-term goal, no fixed date yet. The Hörnli Ridge is the classic line, but it's a serious exposed scramble/climb, not a walk-up — loose rock, fixed ropes in places, and real consequences for being slow. Almost certainly a guided climb given the terrain and the crowding/rockfall issues on the route.

## Open questions

- Guide booking (Zermatt guides book up early in season)
- Rock scrambling/mixed climbing prep — different skill set than the glacier volcanoes
- Season window and rockfall/ice conditions on the ridge
