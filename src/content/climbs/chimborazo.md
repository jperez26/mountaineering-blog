## The plan

Long-term goal, no fixed date yet. Chimborazo is a serious step up from Cotopaxi — higher, colder, and with real rockfall/objective hazard on the standard route depending on conditions. Likely the second Ecuador objective, after Cotopaxi and/or Cayambe build up glacier legs.

## Open questions

- Rockfall conditions on El Corredor (route has shifted over the years as glaciers recede)
- Acclimatization plan — probably needs a few days in the Ecuador highlands first
- Guide selection given the technical/hazard reputation
