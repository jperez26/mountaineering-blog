## The plan

Long-term goal, no fixed date yet. Cayambe sees far fewer climbers than Cotopaxi, with a more broken, crevasse-heavy glacier that demands solid rope-team skills. A good pairing with Cotopaxi on the same Ecuador trip, or a standalone objective.

## Open questions

- Whether to combine with Cotopaxi on one trip or split across visits
- Crevasse conditions / need for a stronger rope team given the glacier's reputation
- Guide vs. independent, given how much quieter this one is than Cotopaxi
