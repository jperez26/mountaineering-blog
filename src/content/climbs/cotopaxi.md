## The plan

Long-term goal, no fixed date yet. Cotopaxi is the natural entry point into the Ecuador volcanoes — a well-guided, well-established glacier route with a hut partway up, which makes it a good next step in altitude and technical difficulty after the Cascades/Mexico climbs.
